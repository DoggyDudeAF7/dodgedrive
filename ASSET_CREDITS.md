# Train asset credits

- **Train Kit** by Kenney — CC0 1.0 Universal. Used for the high-speed, metro, intercity, and diesel freight trains. Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/train-kit

- **Comeng Train** by Danza Entertainment — downloaded from SketchUp 3D Warehouse and incorporated into this game as a modified combined work under the 3D Warehouse General Model License. Source: https://3dwarehouse.sketchup.com/model/5a4cd40621c8c4c27f71a5f4e5fb4fe5/Comeng-Train

The game keeps its lightweight procedural Comeng as a fallback for browsers that cannot load the detailed USDZ asset. The source model is one carriage; the game repeats that carriage to form a consist.
